# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters, readers, and peers who want to quickly understand Pranavi Kondapalli's identity, AI experience, research background, technical projects, and ways to connect. Visitors may arrive with limited time, so the first screen needs to make the name, current experience, and contact routes immediately clear.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Pranavi's experience, projects, research, and contact options through a Spotify-inspired desktop interface. It exists to make a technical portfolio feel familiar, personal, and memorable by letting visitors browse work the way they might browse an artist profile, library, playlist, or currently playing track.

Success means visitors can recognize Pranavi by name within 10 seconds, understand the AI and Carnegie Mellon context, scan experience and projects without friction, and choose the contact path that fits them.

## Positioning

Pranavi's portfolio turns AI research, systems projects, and professional experience into a Spotify-like artist profile that feels familiar while standing apart from conventional portfolios.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: Email, resume, GitHub, and LinkedIn are all first-class choices rather than a single forced action.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Pranavi Kondapalli is an AI-focused Carnegie Mellon student with visible research, project, and contact paths.
- Belief ladder: This is Pranavi's portfolio; she works in AI at Carnegie Mellon; she has credible research, project, and software experience; her work is easy to inspect; contacting or learning more is straightforward.
- Proof on hand: Project repositories, resume PDF, CMU affiliation, research lab and internship entries, visible project imagery, and external profile links already present in the site.

## Brand Personality

Spotify-inspired, dark, sleek, and clean. The interface should feel familiar to anyone who knows Spotify's desktop UI, while still feeling personal to Pranavi through the artist-profile framing, project-as-album treatment, music controls, personal imagery, and interactive browsing details.

## Anti-references

This should not feel like a generic personal portfolio, a resume pasted onto a page, a conventional grid of project cards, or a template site with detached visual decoration. It should not abandon the Spotify angle. Color changes must be discussed with Pranavi before implementation; the current palette and Spotify-like green accent are part of the identity unless she explicitly approves a change.

## Design Principles

The first viewport must identify Pranavi, her AI and CMU context, and direct contact options without making visitors search.

Spotify familiarity is the organizing metaphor: navigation, library, playback, artist profile, albums, track lists, and now-playing affordances should do real portfolio work rather than exist as decoration.

Choices should stay visible. Resume, email, GitHub, and LinkedIn are all valid next actions, and the design should let visitors choose based on their intent.

Stand out through interaction and framing, not novelty for its own sake. The portfolio should feel distinctive because the concept is carried through consistently.

Keep the interface sleek and clean. Dense content is acceptable when it matches Spotify's desktop rhythm, but the hierarchy must remain scannable for recruiters, readers, and peers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard access for navigation, search, controls, carousel actions, external links, and detail panels. Maintain readable contrast, support reduced motion, avoid color-only state communication, and keep contact actions accessible by text or clear labels as well as icons.
